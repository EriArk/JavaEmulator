## Limits and Availability

No new claim for audio, Android 16, physical controller ergonomics or complete
campaign/save compatibility. Symbian/N-Gage remains experimental, including the
known Asphalt 3 cold-relaunch problem. Version-2 phone backups require this
implementation or newer; version-1 backups still restore.
