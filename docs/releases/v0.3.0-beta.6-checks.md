## Limits

Audio is not time-stretched and was not listening-tested. Percentage speed is
guest-clock control, not a universal CPU/frame/audio pacing override. Complete
campaigns, broad save compatibility, Android 16 and physical-phone ergonomics
were not verified. Native Asphalt cold relaunch remains unreliable.
