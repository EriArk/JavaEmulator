# Castle Smasher on early iPhone OS

## Build and library

The private combined ARM64 APK updated AbyssME in place on a Retroid Pocket Classic running Android 14. Its SHA-256 was `f574d025c0ae055b8e28cfa7d9ee86a9cdf17c16a9b5a6a48ee6cbf26dd2709f`. The 4,913,949-byte Castle Smasher 2.01 IPA had SHA-256 `0267a24ebb5cebaa955a8ce79f6bcb15c1f1358f68a13eef5901ce3e359ba016`. touchHLE loaded the ARMv6 executable in the iPhone OS 2.2.1 bundle `com.donutgames.castlesmasher` and selected a landscape OpenGL ES 1.1 context. The [module instructions](../../engines/iphone/README.md) identify the source and resources.

The Android open-file action imported the IPA and started the game. A cold launcher start showed the iPhone OS sticker on the platform grid. Its tab showed the installed card and the original decoded game icon. Reopening the IPA reused the card. All 67 previously recorded managed card paths remained, and the IPA brought the count to 68. Counts cover files matching the managed-card convention.

## Retroid observations

- Reached Start, Challenges, The Wall and its tutorial. An Android-injected touch selected an angle, then held and released Fire. The camera followed the projectile and the remaining stones changed from ten to nine.
- Injected D-pad and A events through the attached Xbox Wireless Controller input node. The cursor moved to Fire and the next projectile reduced the remaining stones to eight. Changed B to Touch in the shared mapper, kept the profile and fired another stone with B. The saved mapper showed the assignment after reinstall and cold launch. Inputs were injected on the device. Physical button feel and simultaneous finger gestures were not assessed.
- Recorded eleven seconds of lossless Android output spanning gameplay and shots. FFmpeg measured −34.5 dB mean and −8.8 dB peak. This measures digital output before the handheld speakers.
- Opened the shared menu during play, enabled CRT and kept it. The display menu retained CRT after an in-place APK update. Restored the original display setting afterward.
- Switched off Music in the guest's options, returned to the game and exited via the host's confirmation. The guest wrote its 1,494-byte `Documents/settings.bin` and a preferences plist. After the update and a cold launch its Music toggle still showed Off and Sound Effects showed On. Re-enabled music and exited normally.
- All 16 tests in LibraryTabsTest, HandheldCycleTest and PlatformControlsTest passed on the final APK. The populated-library test exported and restored packages and guest files to an isolated directory, comparing the source and restored hashes. The original library stayed on the device.

The game, private APK, screenshots, capture and detailed logs remain local under the maintainer build directories. This check covers one landscape game through the first challenge. Other games, live guest orientation changes, hardware sensors and multi-finger gameplay require separate evidence. No public APK or website change was made.
