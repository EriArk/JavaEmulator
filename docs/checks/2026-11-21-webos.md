## Library and shared host

- Opened both IPK container variants through Android and launched their managed copies. The backup test compared packages and per-game data against an isolated restored library. All 53 previous managed card paths survived the update; four webOS games brought the count to 57. The sampled MRE checkpoint retained SHA-256 `8e87a4db3ed3988cacd782b73ef2213b9ebb08c95871fff6507e62202858f5a8`.
- Eleven instrumentation tests passed on the final APK (LibraryTabsTest and HandheldCycleTest). The suite verified tab navigation, IPK versus ZIP identity, folder indexing and an isolated backup/restore. The Retroid finished on the Palm webOS library tab showing four cards.
- The shared virtual display also rendered the Micro Invaders Flash title on the device after the refactor.
- Touch and controller events were injected. No hands-on controller test or full playthrough was performed. Android sound was recorded for Far Out Fowl. The test does not establish audio support for every game or reproduce Palm's original feedback recordings.
- Build output, device screenshots, lossless Android audio capture and instrumentation results are local under `build/checks/webos/`.
