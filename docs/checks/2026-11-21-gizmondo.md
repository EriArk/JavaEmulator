# Carmageddon beta on Gizmondo, 2026-11-21

## Package and build

The supplied English beta is a 35,874,483-byte SD card ZIP, SHA-256 `81a14f730ff08073e8799f31b2b93c18de96bed0b8646de800df9f912e4ccf55`. The card contains a 320 × 240 game for Windows CE ARM, a separate autorun program, a bitmap and the `gzga200036` directory with the original serial marker and packed resources. The main `carmageddon.exe` SHA-256 is `c89938cfa447f2154c9bf70fe31788406c7b50be638ef184d783708eddab2115`. The test used the archive intact.

The Pocket PC 0.3.2 CLI recognized the game executable and screen size but produced black frames and stopped on a guest memory read before the menu. The optional Gizmondo module uses a separately pinned PocketHLE Reborn v0.4.9 build. The [build instructions](../../engines/gizmondo/README.md) list its features, shared settings and hardware coverage. The final APK SHA-256 is `f12487340a80cd20fb8170f692b8d9f5d8761d35a7799c54ae265163ac38e4ed`. It updated the combined private ARM64 test build in place. All 65 previous managed cards remained and the Gizmondo card brought the count to 66. Games, native binaries, the test APK and original capture files are local.

## Retroid Pocket Classic, Android 14

- The Android open-file action recognized the card, launched the language picker and selected the game executable automatically. English, New Game, Max Damage, difficulty selection, Main Street and Red Eagle led to a rendered race with traffic, the timer, speedometer and damage indicators. The speedometer reached 82 MPH on the final build. The race timed out and the game returned to track selection.
- Input injected through the connected Xbox controller selected menu items, steered and triggered repairs. A press on the on-screen Fire key opened Sound Settings. The shared controller and display panels, temporary on-screen control preview, and exit through the guest's own Quit menu were exercised. No hand-operated controller trial or full career playthrough was performed.
- The game uses a live mono WinMM ring containing 2,048 PCM samples at 11,050 Hz. The old Android feeder consumed 2,048 samples per pull. The guest's modulo cursor repeatedly returned to the same point and its buffer remained silent. With pulls of about 10 ms, guest mixer updates became observable. Android lossless playback capture measured a peak of −2.5 dB in the menus and 0 dB during the race. The prior 12-second race capture contained digital silence. The final clean build also produced non-silent menu audio (−16.6 dB mean over ten seconds).
- The game wrote a 3,648-byte file under its emulated flash disk. After lowering Effects Volume, quitting through the guest menu and installing the final APK, the new session showed the same reduced volume. The test restored full effects volume afterward. The initial unfinished race left Continue Career disabled.

The final Android instrumentation run passed all 13 LibraryTabsTest and HandheldCycleTest cases, including card routing and an isolated restore of the populated library. Age of Empires also launched through the Pocket PC host after the shared PCM change. Its seven-second Android playback capture measured −27.9 dB mean and −13.9 dB peak.

Local logs, before/after audio and screenshots are under `build/checks/gizmondo/`. Compatibility with other Gizmondo games, camera and GPS titles and static video playback remains unverified.
