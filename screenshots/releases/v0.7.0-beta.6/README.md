# Beta 6 new-features demo

[Watch or download the video](abyssme-beta6-new-features.mp4) — 58 seconds,
1.9 MB, 720 × 960, H.264 at 30 fps, no audio track.

Recorded on November 14, 2026 on a real Retroid Pocket Classic running Android
14 and the official signed **0.7.0-beta.6 / 320 ARM64** APK.

The recording shows creating the named **Racing** profile, selecting Gamepad,
adding two extra buttons, assigning keys 5 and 6, moving them and adjusting
opacity, saving the profile, and assigning it to Asphalt through the game
selection UI. Resetting that game's previous touch/LCD overrides is explicitly
selected and confirmed. The final section shows Asphalt 3 Street Rules 3D 1.1.5
with those extra controls and injected input during a race.

Android screenrecord captured the device directly, with ADB-injected touch
input and visible touch indicators. The edit joins selected passages at normal
speed, removes navigation/loading pauses and uses a second gameplay take.
English captions are added beneath the original screen image; screens are not
recreated. The video is silent. Games and their artwork are user supplied;
no game binaries or firmware are included in this repository media.

The temporary profile, game-control changes and device recording settings were
removed/restored after capture. This short demonstration does not establish
complete-game, physical-button comfort, audible-output or ARMv7 acceptance.
See the separate [release checks](../../../docs/releases/v0.7.0-beta.6-checks.md).

The final 1,735-frame MP4 was decoded end to end without errors. Representative
editor, assignment, gameplay and caption frames were inspected. Raw recordings,
edit timing, encoder metadata and cleanup evidence are retained locally under
`build/checks/2026-11-14-demo/` and are not public assets.
