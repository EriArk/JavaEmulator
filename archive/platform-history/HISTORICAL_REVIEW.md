# Historical fact-check of AbyssME platform cards

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-22  
**Coverage:** 32 existing platform entries, 8 translations per entry.  
**Scope:** Core historical claims, model identity, device-vs-software attribution, launch chronology, standout hardware features, and obvious contradictions in localized copy. This is a **source-backed editorial pass, not an independent museum certification or a native-speaker review of every sentence**.

The content stays \`draft-for-review\` until final image-rights/source review and in-app integration. No APK was changed.

## Localization and data-quality fixes

The same review also uncovered a **locale-indexing bug** in the existing shared glossary. Descriptive specifications and period labels had shifted to the next language, leaving the simplified-Chinese versions empty. This was repaired for **all 27 specification phrases and 3 date-period phrases across the eight locale keys**. The previously missing `DE` country entry (Germany) and the new `RU` entry (Russia) were completed for all eight languages.

The collection's validator now checks actual country/date label coverage and **nonempty translations for every glossary entry in every supported locale**, rather than merely checking that a language object exists. The historical articles themselves were not misindexed; this affected their short fact labels and descriptions.

## Editorial rules for future cards

1. **Label the year carefully.** Prefer the year the pictured *device first shipped*. If the first prototype, announcement, crowdfunding, local pilot and international launch differ, name the event explicitly in the article.
2. **Separate device maker, platform developer and operating-system vendor.** The title/photo can depict a representative device even when the platform covers many manufacturers or release generations.
3. **Do not manufacture precision.** A screen dimension, CPU revision, release date or sales figure that disagrees between otherwise plausible references should be omitted or described qualitatively until confirmed by documentation or physical evidence.
4. **One known game's successful run does not establish the platform's complete library compatibility.** Historical cards describe the actual hardware and its era, not current emulator compatibility.
5. **Keep historical links, illustration credits and limitations alongside each card.** A valid URL is not proof every claim is correct; provenance may still need a better primary source, especially for obscure devices.
6. **Translate corrections semantically.** When a factual claim changes, review all eight published language variants. Maker names, physical model details and standard technology labels should not be silently translated into different facts.
7. **Do a final native-language proofread and image-license clearance before shipping.** This pass checks factual consistency, but does not certify idiomatic accuracy across all eight languages or the right to redistribute every derivative image.

**Result:** The articles retain their short GT7-like style, while the most consequential misleading metadata and chronology have been corrected. The collection is ready for a future UI/content review, **not yet an audited museum exhibit or finished user-facing APK feature**.
