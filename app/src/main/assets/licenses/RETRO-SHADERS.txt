shaders/retro/crt-easymode.glsl
Upstream: crt/shaders/crt-easymode.glsl
CRT Shader by EasyMode. Upstream declaration: "License: GPL".
For this distribution we follow GPLv3 (unspecified-version option, section 14).
The original notice and parameters remain in the shader.

xBRZ adaptation (2026): ABYSS_XBRZ_ANALYZE emits four packed per-corner
decisions at guest resolution. shaders/retro/xbrz-resolve.glsl is derived from
the retained get_left_ratio and ordered blend formulas and applies those
decisions at output resolution. The default branch retains the original
single-pass algorithm for comparison and small scales. No thresholds/default
parameters were changed. The new adapter/resolve work is GPL-3.0-only.
CRT EasyMode's body and defaults are unchanged. AbyssME's GLES2 adapter supplies
stage defines, uniforms, guest-size cropping and framebuffer presentation.
Neither shader is chained with FSR, interpolation or LCD effects.
Complete GPLv3 terms are included in this assets/licenses directory.
